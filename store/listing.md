## Long description (paste as plain text; the store does not render markdown)

The news is a lot. Rose Colored Glasses rewrites the headlines on your favorite news sites into happier versions, right on the page, while everything else stays exactly where it was. Click "Restore originals" any time and the real news comes back.

No account. No API key. No setup. Install it, open a news site, watch the headlines flip.

PICK YOUR FLAVOR
Ten humor styles: Wholesome, Dry, Absurd, Dad jokes, Unhinged optimism, Beautiful feet enjoyer, Squirrel misadventures, Shakespearean, Infomercial pitchman, and Midwest nice.

Then set the sarcasm slider: 0 is sincere sunshine, 10 is the full Onion. The popup's rose tint deepens as you slide. Flip on "Fully checked out" and facts become optional.

Headlines rewrite automatically as you browse (you can turn that off), including new ones that load as you scroll. A counter on the icon shows how many headlines got happier on the current page.

WORKS ON
AP News, Google News, CNN, NPR, Reuters, The New York Times, The Washington Post, Yahoo News, NBC News, Fox News, USA Today, ABC News, CBS News, CNBC, Bloomberg, The Wall Street Journal, Politico, Axios, The Hill, LA Times, Time, BBC, The Guardian, Al Jazeera, Sky News, The Independent, The Telegraph, CBC, ABC Australia, The Texas Tribune, Houston Chronicle, The Dallas Morning News, Express-News, and the Austin American-Statesman.

Reading something else? Open the popup and click "Enable on this site" — it asks Chrome for permission for that one site only, and you can remove it any time from the options page.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Headline text is the only page content that ever leaves your browser, and a random install ID enforces a fair daily limit — it identifies no one. If your device supports Chrome's built-in on-device AI, rewrites happen entirely on your machine and nothing leaves your browser at all. No accounts, no analytics, nothing sold. Full privacy policy: https://johnfutia99.github.io/rose-colored-glasses/privacy.html

The daily rewrite allowance is free and resets every day. When it runs out, the extension tells you nicely and leaves the page alone.
